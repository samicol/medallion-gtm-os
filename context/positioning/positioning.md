## Category

## Target Customer

Healthcare organizations that employ or manage credentialed providers and are in active growth mode:
- Digital health and telehealth companies (VC-backed, multi-state, Series A–C)
- Multi-site provider groups (PE-backed or private, 50–1,000+ providers, clinic expansion)
- Home care and in-home medical groups (risk-bearing, complex multi-payer enrollment)
- Health systems seeking cost reduction and compliance automation

Source: context/icp/icp.md

## Unique Value

**Medallion is the only platform that combines AI-native credentialing, multi-state licensing, and payer enrollment in a single system built for healthcare organizations in growth mode.**

The differentiation from legacy alternatives:
- **Built for speed, not just compliance:** Competitors (symplr, VerityStream) optimize for large-system compliance; Medallion optimizes for time-to-billing at growth stage
- **AI-native, not AI-added:** Built from scratch as an AI platform; legacy platforms have retrofitted automation onto 15-year-old architectures
- **End-to-end, not point solution:** Covers credentialing + licensing + enrollment + monitoring in one platform, not four separate tools
- **Performance guarantees:** SLAs written into contracts — unusual for the category

## Positioning Statement

Medallion is the AI-powered provider operations platform for healthcare organizations that are growing their clinical workforce. Unlike legacy credentialing software built for large hospitals, Medallion automates credentialing, multi-state licensing, and payer enrollment in a single AI-native platform — turning a 90-day bottleneck into a 6-day process, so providers reach patients faster and organizations start billing sooner.
