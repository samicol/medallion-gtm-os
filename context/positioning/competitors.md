## Where Medallion Wins

1. **Speed:** 1-day credentialing file readiness vs. 60–120 day industry average; 6-day time-to-billing vs. months
2. **Growth-stage fit:** Legacy platforms are designed for hospitals with stable rosters; Medallion is designed for companies scaling fast
3. **End-to-end:** Credentialing + licensing + enrollment + monitoring in one platform; competitors typically cover a subset
4. **AI-native:** Built from scratch as an AI platform; competitors have retrofitted AI onto legacy architectures
5. **Performance guarantees:** SLAs in contracts; legacy platforms don't do this

Source: context/docs/research/competitors.md, company.md

---

## Where Medallion Loses

1. **EHR integration depth:** VerityStream integrates natively with Epic, Cerner, Workday; Medallion's EHR integration depth not confirmed from public sources
2. **Enterprise install base:** symplr and VerityStream are deeply embedded in large health systems; switching cost is high for established accounts
3. **Brand recognition in legacy health systems:** Legacy platforms have 10–20 year relationships with hospital procurement teams
4. **INFERRED:** Pricing may be higher than legacy platforms for large organizations that just need basic credentialing; Medallion's AI value is clearest at growth stage

## How to Respond to Competitor Mentions

**"We already use symplr / VerityStream":**
- Acknowledge their investment; ask what it costs them per credentialing file and how long payer enrollment takes today
- Medallion's ROI story (Tampa General $2.4M, WellBe 80% time reduction) is the counter
- Not worth pursuing unless they're in growth mode and hitting speed/scale problems

**"We were looking at Assured":**
- Medallion has more funding, more customers (300+), and CredAlliance as a unique differentiation
- Ask what their primary need is — if it's pure automation, Medallion should win on features and scale

**"CAQH handles this for us":**
- CAQH is a data repository, not a workflow tool; it doesn't automate enrollment, licensing, or monitoring
- Medallion uses CAQH data as an input; they're complementary
