## What they sell

Medallion is an AI-powered provider operations platform that automates credentialing, licensing, payer enrollment, and provider data management for healthcare organizations. It describes itself as "healthcare's first real-time CVO" (Credentials Verification Organization) and, after acquiring Andros, "the largest AI-native credentialing platform in the country."
Source: https://medallion.co | Checked: 2026-11-13

**Core products/solutions:**
- **Provider Enrollment** — submits and tracks payer enrollment across Medicare, Medicaid, and commercial payers; AI agents handle prep, submission, and follow-up simultaneously
- **CVO Credentialing** — NCQA-aligned credentials verification; average 1-day file readiness, 99.9% accuracy (self-reported)
- **Cross-State Licensing** — tracks and renews provider licenses across all required states; designed for telehealth companies serving multi-state patients
- **Provider Data Management** — central, continuously verified source of truth for all provider data, feeding credentialing, enrollment, and monitoring
- **Ongoing Monitoring** — continuous compliance monitoring; one source cites a 30-day review cycle requirement for all files processed after July 1, 2025 (verify against CMS)
- **Roster Management** — manages provider rosters at scale
- **Privileging** — hospital and system privileging workflows
- **Delegated Credentialing** — credentialing on behalf of payer partners
- **CredAlliance** — shared credentialing clearinghouse launched August 2025; verifies a provider once and shares results with participating payer networks, eliminating ~$1.2B in annual duplicated verification costs (company estimate)
Source: https://medallion.co/news/medallion-raises-43-million-to-expand-ai-infrastructure-and-launch-credalliance | Checked: 2026-11-13
