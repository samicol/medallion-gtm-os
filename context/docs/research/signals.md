# Buying Signals Research: Medallion

> Public evidence of what makes a company need Medallion now. Sources cited throughout.

---

## Signal 1: Company is hiring providers at scale

**Mother intent:** Scaling a provider workforce = scaling credentialing/enrollment workload
**Why it creates need:** Every new provider hire triggers a licensing and payer enrollment process. At 10+ providers, manual management breaks. At 50+, it becomes a full-time operations problem.

**Where to detect it:**
- LinkedIn job postings for "NP", "PA", "MD", "therapist", "psychiatrist", "nurse practitioner", etc.
- LinkedIn headcount growth in clinical roles
- Indeed/ZipRecruiter postings
- Company press releases announcing provider network expansion

**Qualifying filter:** Companies hiring 5+ clinical providers in the last 90 days, especially across multiple states
Source: https://medallion.co/who-we-serve/digital-health | Checked: 2026-11-13

---

## Signal 2: Company raised a funding round (Series A or later)

**Mother intent:** New capital = new growth plan = new provider hiring = new credentialing volume
**Why it creates need:** Series A+ digital health companies typically have aggressive provider expansion plans in the 12 months post-funding. They've outgrown spreadsheets and need infrastructure.

**Where to detect it:**
- Crunchbase, PitchBook, TechCrunch, Axios Pro Rata
- LinkedIn announcements
- Press releases

**Qualifying filter:** B2B healthcare companies (telehealth, digital health, home care, behavioral health) raising $5M+
Source: INFERRED from Ginger case study (raised $1B+ round, used Medallion) and Medallion's investor messaging
Source: https://www.medallion.co/resources/case-studies/how-ginger-scaled-credentialing | Checked: 2026-11-13

---

## Signal 3: Company is expanding into new states

**Mother intent:** Multi-state expansion = multi-state licensing requirements for every provider
**Why it creates need:** A telehealth company expanding from 5 states to 20 states multiplies its licensing workload 4x overnight. Without automation, this requires 4x the operations headcount.

**Where to detect it:**
- Press releases: "expanding to [state]", "now serving patients in [state]"
- Job postings in new geographic markets
- LinkedIn company updates about geographic expansion
- Product/app store listings showing expanded service areas

**Qualifying filter:** Digital health or telehealth companies announcing service in 3+ new states in a single press release, or job postings in 3+ new states simultaneously
Source: https://medallion.co/who-we-serve/digital-health | Checked: 2026-11-13

---

## Signal 4: Company is hiring a VP/Director of Credentialing or Provider Operations

**Mother intent:** Hiring a credentialing leader = the problem has become painful enough to need a dedicated owner
**Why it creates need:** At this stage, companies either hire a credentialing ops leader AND buy software, or they buy software to avoid hiring the full team. Medallion wins by offering automation that reduces the team needed.

**Where to detect it:**
- LinkedIn job postings for "Director of Credentialing", "VP Provider Operations", "Head of Provider Success", "Manager of Provider Enrollment"
- Indeed/ZipRecruiter

**Qualifying filter:** Digital health, telehealth, or home care companies posting these roles, especially if the company has fewer than 50 employees (signal they're in growth mode)
Source: INFERRED from Medallion's digital health page pain points ("scale efficiently without increasing headcount")

---

## Signal 5: Company is posting provider roles AND credentialing/operations roles simultaneously

**Mother intent:** Operational strain — growing clinical team is outpacing operations capacity
**Why it creates need:** Posting both clinical and operational roles simultaneously is the strongest indicator of a credentialing bottleneck in progress.

**Where to detect it:**
- LinkedIn: company has open roles for providers + credentialing/enrollment staff in the same 30-day window

**Qualifying filter:** Digital health company with 10–500 employees, posting both provider and operations/credentialing roles in the same 30 days
Source: INFERRED from Medallion's customer base and product positioning

---

## Signal 6: Company has payer enrollment-related job postings

**Mother intent:** Payer enrollment is a bottleneck — the company needs to get providers billing and is struggling
**Why it creates need:** Posting for a "Payer Enrollment Specialist" or "Credentialing Coordinator" means the company is trying to solve the problem manually. Medallion's pitch is: buy software instead of building a team.

**Where to detect it:**
- LinkedIn, Indeed: job titles containing "payer enrollment", "credentialing coordinator", "provider enrollment", "CAQH"

**Qualifying filter:** Digital health or home care companies with under 300 employees posting for these roles
Source: INFERRED from Medallion's product positioning and competitive differentiation
