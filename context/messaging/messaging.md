# Messaging: Medallion

> Built from research files only. Every claim traces to context/docs/research/voice.md, company.md, customers.md.
> No em dashes. No invented numbers.

---

## Core Message

**Medallion turns provider onboarding into a revenue engine — not a 90-day wait.**

Every day a provider is credentialed but not yet enrolled with payers is revenue your organization is not collecting. Medallion's AI platform handles credentialing, multi-state licensing, and payer enrollment in parallel, automatically, so providers go from hired to billing in days instead of months.

---

## Message Pillars

### Pillar 1: Speed to revenue

**Claim:** Providers reach patients and start billing faster.
**Proof points:**
- Average credentialing file ready in 1 day (Medallion self-reported)
- WellBe Senior Medical: turnaround now 6 days vs. industry average of 60–120 days
- Estimated 2x faster time-to-revenue (Medallion self-reported)
**Angle for ops persona:** "Your credentialing backlog is a billing backlog."
**Angle for CFO persona:** "Every 30 days of enrollment delay on a $200K provider is roughly $16K in unbillable care." (INFERRED calculation — do not use without verifying exact figure with Medallion)

### Pillar 2: Scale without headcount

**Claim:** Organizations grow their provider roster without growing their ops team at the same rate.
**Proof points:**
- Ivy Rehab: 700+ clinics, no headcount increase in credentialing ops
- CareBridge: Scaled multi-state licensing without building a larger internal team
- 66% estimated reduction in administrative costs (Medallion self-reported)
**Angle for ops persona:** "You shouldn't need to hire a credentialing coordinator for every 20 providers."
**Angle for growth persona:** "Your operations team is a fixed cost. Your provider network should not be."

### Pillar 3: Compliance without manual review

**Claim:** Organizations stay audit-ready and compliant without spreadsheet tracking or manual monitoring.
**Proof points:**
- 99.9% average file accuracy (Medallion self-reported)
- PM Pediatric Care: 50% reduction in credentialing denials
- Real-time dashboards for credentialing, enrollment, and monitoring
- NCQA-aligned CVO process (verify NCQA status directly with Medallion)
**Angle for CCO/GC persona:** "One lapsed license on a telehealth encounter is a liability event. Real-time monitoring removes the guesswork."

### Pillar 4: Built for how healthcare actually grows

**Claim:** Medallion is designed for organizations adding providers across multiple states, not for hospitals with stable rosters.
**Proof points:**
- Multi-state licensing automated for digital health customers (Iris Telehealth, FOLX, Antidote Health)
- CredAlliance clearinghouse eliminates duplicate verification across payers
- AI agents trained on evolving payer rules and state licensing requirements
**Angle for digital health persona:** "You're in 5 states today. When you're in 20, your spreadsheet won't work. Here's what does."

## Words to Avoid

- Em dashes (rule in CLAUDE.md)
- "Best-in-class" or "industry-leading" without proof
- "AI-powered" as a standalone claim (everyone says this; pair with a specific outcome)
- "Streamline" (generic; replace with a specific time or cost claim)
- "Revolutionize" or "transform" (inflated; Medallion uses measured language)
- Generic healthcare jargon without patient or provider context
- Invented numbers (INFERRED calculations must be flagged before use in copy)

---

## Messaging Don'ts

- Do not claim specific dollar amounts or percentages without tracing them to customers.md or facts.md
- Do not name a competitor negatively in cold outreach (mention internally but not in emails)
- Do not raise compliance risks as a first-touch topic to ops personas (leads with fear; save for CCO persona)
- Do not ask for a demo in Email 1 (brief says ask for a 15-minute call, not a demo)
