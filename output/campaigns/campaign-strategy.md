## Ranking criteria
1. **Signal strength** — how directly does the trigger indicate a need for Medallion right now?
2. **Data ease** — how clean and accessible is the data source? (1=hard/scrape, 5=clean API)
3. **Volume** — approximate companies per month matching the signal (INFERRED)
4. **Angle quality** — how specific and non-generic is the outreach story?

## Tier Definitions

| Tier | Criteria |
|---|---|
| **Tier 1** | Signal is real-time, detectable programmatically, high volume, and maps directly to an immediate Medallion need. Build first. |
| **Tier 2** | Signal is good but either lower volume, harder data, or slightly more indirect connection to the need. Build after Tier 1 workflows are running. |
| **Tier 3** | Speculative, event-driven, or harder to detect reliably. Run as experiments after Tier 1 and 2 are validated. |
